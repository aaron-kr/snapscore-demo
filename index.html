How it works

  1. Pick an answer key on the left (or add one: paste it, drop a CSV, or a Google Sheets link).
  2. Drop the scans here. They are listed at once and graded automatically.
  3. Click any row to see the marked sheet. Fix anything unclear there.
  4. Copy the scores into your gradebook, or download the marked sheets to send to students.
